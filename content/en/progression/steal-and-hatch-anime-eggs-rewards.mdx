## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the best Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs rewards to aim for early on?
In early zones, prioritize Ninja and Saiyan eggs. These offer a balanced combination of fast incubation times and solid rare/epic drop rates that will significantly increase your farming speed without requiring high rebirth stats.

### Can you lose your egg if you are knocked out while carrying it?
Yes. If an enemy player or NPC defender defeats you while transporting an egg, it will drop to the ground and can be picked up by anyone nearby. Always plan your escape route before lifting rare eggs.

### How do luck boosts affect Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs rewards?
Luck boosts directly shift the drop weight table toward Epic, Legendary, and Mythic rarities. While they do not guarantee a Mythic unit, running a 2x Luck Boost during a server-wide event dramatically increases your odds on premium eggs.

### Is it better to sell duplicate units or fuse them?
In almost all progression stages, fusing duplicates is better than selling them. Fusing creates Shiny and Golden variations that yield higher passive income and combat stats than base units.
