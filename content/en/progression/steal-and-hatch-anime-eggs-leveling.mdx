## Core Progression Loop: How Leveling Works

At its heart, the gameplay revolves around a high-stakes heist cycle. You sneak into dangerous themed zones while massive anime bosses sleep, grab an egg, and sprint back to your home plot before the alarm alerts everyone and kicks off a frantic chase. Once you safely deposit the egg onto your plot, you hatch it to obtain powerful anime-themed companions that boost your stats and income generation.

```
[Infiltrate Boss Zone] ➔ [Steal Anime Egg] ➔ [Escape Alarm Chase] ➔ [Place & Hatch Pet] ➔ [Reinvest Coins into Upgrades]
```

To accelerate your growth, you must reinvest your earnings into specific base upgrades:

- **Treadmill Training:** Increases your base movement speed and sprint stamina, allowing you to survive higher-tier zone alarms.
- **Pen Upgrades:** Expands your plot capacity, unlocks faster hatching timers, and boosts passive coin multipliers from placed pets.
- **Trails & Cosmetics:** Grants flat speed bonuses and jump height enhancements.
- **Rebirth Multipliers:** Resets base currency in exchange for permanent percentage stat boosts across all systems.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest method for Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs leveling?
The fastest leveling method is maintaining an aggressive rebirth schedule while keeping your best multiplier pets equipped. Focus on upgrading your treadmill enough to reliably escape Zone 3 and Zone 4, then reinvest your high-tier egg earnings into expanding your pen capacity.

### Do I lose my hatched pets when I rebirth?
No. Rebirthing resets your raw coin balance, pen upgrades, and basic treadmill tiers, but your unlocked pet collection, index milestones, and permanent rebirth multipliers carry over.

### Which upgrades should I prioritize first: Treadmill or Pen?
In the early game, balance both equally. Upgrade your treadmill enough to easily outrun the Zone 2 boss, then dump your profits into Pen Level 3 to maximize passive income generation. Once your passive income stabilizes, alternate between speed and capacity upgrades.
