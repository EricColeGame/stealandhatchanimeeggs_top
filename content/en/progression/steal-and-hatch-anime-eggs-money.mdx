## How the Core Income Loop Works

The progression loop centers around high-risk egg runs and automated cash production. Every player follows a four-step cycle: navigate the wilderness, steal an egg from a guarded nest, survive the retreat back to base, and hatch it into a profit-generating pet.

```
[Explore Biome] ➔ [Steal Egg from Nest] ➔ [Dodge Beasts & Players] ➔ [Hatch at Base] ➔ [Collect Passive Money]
```

Every hatched pet stays on your base and yields a set amount of cash per second ($/s). You then reinvest that revenue into upgrading your base facilities and running on treadmills to boost movement speed. Higher speed enables you to reach advanced biomes where higher-yield eggs await.

### Core Progression Pillars

* **Egg Quality:** Higher biomes feature nests with rarer egg drops, yielding significantly higher base cash flow.
* **Movement Speed:** Treadmill upgrades let you outrun aggressive nest beasts and evade tackling players who try to steal your loot.
* **Pet Index Bonuses:** Hatching unique species completes the Pet Index, providing permanent account-wide income and agility multipliers.
* **Pet Mutations and Sizing:** According to community reports, larger pet sizes and special modifiers dramatically amplify base earnings per second.

## Actionable Strategies to Maximize Earnings Fast

To scale your cash reserves smoothly, follow these community-tested operational habits:

### 1. Clear Biomes in Strict Sequential Order
Many players waste time attempting high-tier runs before their base stats are ready. Instead of dying in the Volcano or Prehistoric zones, complete lower biomes first. Filling out the Pet Index gives baseline speed and cash multipliers that make harder biomes significantly easier.

### 2. Prioritize Speed Over Raw Nest Searching
Speed is the true gating mechanic in the game. Always balance pet hatching with treadmill training. If you cannot reliably outrun nest beasts or dodge aggressive players looking to tackle you, your survival rate drops, stalling your cash generation.

### 3. Build a Stash for Rift Sacrifices
Never immediately delete duplicate Rare or Epic pets. The lobby Rift Machine frequently asks for specific lower-tier pets in exchange for high-yield Rift eggs. Keep a reserve so you can claim Rift Eggs instantly when the machine refreshes.
