## Step-by-Step Beginner’s Guide: Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs How to Play

If you are logging in for your first session, following a structured path ensures you do not waste precious starter cash on low-value upgrades.

### 1. Infiltrate the Starter Boss Territory
Head out from the central hub into the designated starter world. Each world contains a central boss guarding clusters of eggs. Keep your distance until you spot an opening, then quickly interact to grab the egg.

### 2. Evade the Boss Chase
Once you pick up an egg, the boss will immediately begin hunting you down. Your movement speed is critical here. Stick to open pathways, avoid sharp corners where you might get trapped, and cross the boundary line back into your private plot where bosses cannot enter.

### 3. Incubate and Hatch Your Fighter
Drop the egg into your plot's incubator slots. Higher-tier eggs require longer hatch times, but they yield significantly stronger anime heroes. 

### 4. Deploy Fighters for Passive Income
Place your hatched characters on designated stands around your base. Each fighter generates continuous cash per second (CPS), which fuels your infrastructure upgrades.

### 5. Hit the Treadmill to Build Speed
Never neglect your character's base speed. By training on your plot’s treadmill, you permanently increase your sprint velocity, allowing you to raid deeper worlds with faster, deadlier bosses.
