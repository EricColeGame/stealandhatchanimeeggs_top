### How Eggs and Multipliers Work

Every egg in the game possesses distinct attributes that dictate how much passive income the hatched pet yields:

1. **Egg Shape:** Dictates the species and base rarity tier.
2. **Egg Color:** Cosmetic variation only. Color differences affect how the egg and hatched pet look, but community reports confirm colors offer no passive income bonus.
3. **Egg Size:** Applies a direct multiplier to the base coin output of the pet. However, larger eggs add substantial carry weight, significantly slowing down your sprint speed.
4. **Respawn Timers:** Biome nests reset every 5 minutes. If an area has been picked clean by other players, you must wait for the next refresh cycle.

| Mechanic | Impact on Gameplay | Priority for Beginners |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Egg Shape** | Determines base rarity & species | High |
| **Egg Size** | Multiplies pet earnings, adds weight penalty | Very High |
| **Egg Color** | Visual cosmetic flair only | Low |
| **5-Minute Reset** | Clears taken nests & spawns fresh eggs | Medium |

## Speed Training and Guardian Mechanics

The greatest obstacle between you and an Eternal-tier egg is the sleeping guardian monster resting beside the nest. 

### Surviving Guardian Encounters

When you approach an egg nest, nearby guardians will wake up if disturbed. If a guardian catches you, you drop the carried egg and respawn back at your pen. To survive:

* **Train on Treadmills:** Spend downtime running on treadmills to passively boost your top speed.
* **Equip Trails:** Trails grant active multiplier boosts to your acceleration and maximum movement velocity.
* **Calculate Carry Weight:** A massive Divine egg can reduce your speed by up to 50%, making you an easy target for fast guardians.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important tip in this Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs beginner guide?
The most crucial tip is prioritizing speed upgrades through treadmills and trails before attempting runs in higher-tier biomes. Without sufficient base speed, carrying heavy, high-rarity eggs will slow you down too much to escape guardian beasts.

### Do egg colors change how much money my pet produces?
No. Egg colors are purely cosmetic and alter the visual appearance of the egg and hatched pet. The actual income rate is determined by the egg's species (shape/rarity) and its physical size multiplier.

### How often do eggs respawn in biomes?
Eggs reset across all biomes every 5 minutes. If another player steals an egg before you reach the nest, you can wait for the reset timer to trigger a fresh batch of spawns.

### Where can I find Brainrot and Mecha eggs?
Brainrot, Monster, and Mecha eggs do not spawn in standard biome nests. Player experience shows these are obtained exclusively via limited-time events, special server drops, or the in-game store.
