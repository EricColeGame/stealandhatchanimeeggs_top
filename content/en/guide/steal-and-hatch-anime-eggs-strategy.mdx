## Advanced Pro Tips for Heist Efficiency

To maintain peak efficiency throughout your progression, implement these high-level tactics:

*   **Corner Jumping:** Boss pursuers follow fixed pathfinding nodes. Cutting sharp 90-degree corners around zone geometry causes pursuers to lose tracking momentum.
*   **Daily Login Stacking:** Never claim your daily streak reward until you reach your highest unlocked zone for that session, as cash payouts frequently scale with current zone progression.
*   **Index Completion Priority:** Don't sell newly discovered low-tier pets immediately. Hatch them once to log them into your Index for permanent account-wide stat boosts.
*   **Audio Cue Timing:** Keep game audio active. Boss waking animations feature distinct audio cues approximately 1.5 seconds before the chase alarm sounds, giving you an early jump on your escape route.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs strategy for solo players?
The fastest solo **Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs strategy** involves focusing strictly on treadmill upgrades during the first five minutes of each rebirth. High movement speed allows you to safely farm Epic and Legendary eggs in Pirate Cove without triggering chase sequences, accelerating your economy into mid-game zones rapidly.

### How do I stop boss pursuers from catching me in late-game zones?
Equip pets that carry the passive alarm delay trait (such as the Chakra Prodigy archetype) and upgrade your trails. Additionally, practice corner routing around zone obstacles, as bosses suffer speed penalties when recalculating pathfinding around sharp turns.

### When should I sell eggs versus hatching them?
Hatch every egg that has not yet been logged into your collection Index. Once an egg's hatch pool is fully documented in your Index, you should only hatch eggs that match or exceed your current lowest-tier equipped pet; auto-sell everything below that threshold to maximize gold efficiency.
