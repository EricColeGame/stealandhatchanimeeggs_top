## Mastering Movement: Stealing and Escaping on Mobile

The core loop requires you to infiltrate enemy zones, grab valuable eggs, and sprint back to your home base while avoiding pursuit. Doing this on a mobile touchscreen requires distinct mechanical habits.

### 1. The Pre-Grab Camera Angle
Before you tap the egg to steal it, pan your camera 180 degrees backwards using your right thumb. This allows you to immediately see your escape route back to your plot without needing to adjust your perspective while running.

### 2. The Multi-Finger "Claw" Grip Advantage
For competitive players, adopting a 3-finger layout can significantly improve your survival rate:
- **Left Thumb:** Controls the dynamic movement thumbstick.
- **Right Index Finger:** Taps the egg pickup and interaction buttons.
- **Right Thumb:** Controls the camera swipe and jump button.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I fix camera spin issues in Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs mobile?
If your camera spins erratically while playing **Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs mobile**, open your in-game Roblox settings and adjust the **Camera Sensitivity** slider down to between 0.35 and 0.50. Additionally, ensure your touchscreen is clean and free of moisture, which can register false drag inputs.

### Can I play Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs mobile with a Bluetooth controller?
Yes. Roblox natively supports most modern Bluetooth controllers (such as Xbox, PlayStation, and mobile gamepads) on iOS and Android. Connecting a controller automatically remaps movement to physical analog sticks while retaining on-screen UI buttons for menus.

### Why does my character slow down when stealing eggs on mobile?
Carrying certain high-tier anime eggs incurs a weight penalty that lowers your base movement speed. To overcome this, spend more time training on your plot's treadmill and upgrade your treadmill tier to boost your underlying speed multiplier.

### Is mobile performance cross-compatible with PC and console players?
Yes, all worlds, player plots, fighter collections, and leaderboards are completely synchronized across mobile, tablet, desktop, and console platforms. Progress made on your phone automatically saves to your account.
