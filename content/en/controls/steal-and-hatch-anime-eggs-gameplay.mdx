## The Core Loop: Stealing, Securing, and Hatching

Understanding the fundamental rhythm of **Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs gameplay** will help you prioritize your resource investments. The game revolves around a three-step gameplay loop: Infiltration, Transport & Defense, and Incubation.

```
[Infiltrate Base] ➔ [Grab High-Tier Egg] ➔ [Dodge Traps & Sentry] ➔ [Deposit in Incubator] ➔ [Hatch Anime Hero]
```

### 1. Infiltration & Grabbing
Navigate through enemy zones or neutral nests scattered across the map. Higher-tier eggs are placed deeper inside heavily fortified zones. Once you trigger the interaction key, your carry weight increases, which slightly reduces base movement speed depending on the egg weight tier.

### 2. Transport & Hazard Navigation
When returning to your home base, use jump-buffering along elevated rails and avoid active floor traps. Community reports suggest taking winding back-alleys rather than straight center corridors to avoid ranged snare items used by defenders.

### 3. Incubation & Hatching
Place the acquired egg directly into your home incubator. Each egg category possesses a distinct hatch timer that can be accelerated using passive speed boosts, pet synergy buffs, or currency upgrades.

## Advanced Movement Mechanics & Escape Tactics

To excel against experienced opponents, master advanced movement techniques that exploit map geometry and momentum conservation.

### Jump-Buffering and Momentum Preservation
When carrying heavy eggs (Legendary and Mythic), your character suffers from slower acceleration. You can bypass this penalty by initiating a sprint-jump sequence *before* interacting with the egg. If timed correctly, your initial jump carries standard momentum for the first 1.5 seconds of your escape route.

### Wall-Bouncing & Corner Snapping
Roblox collision mechanics allow players to maintain velocity when brushing against angled surfaces. By turning your camera 45 degrees into a wall while holding `W + D` (or `W + A`), your character slides along surfaces faster than a direct forward run, dodging point-blank snare projectiles.

```
       [Wall Surface]
         /
        /  <-- Slide at 45-degree angle
[Player] ➔ ➔ (Maintains max velocity & evades traps)
```

### Baiting Defender Abilities
Never charge straight into a nest. Tap the interact key to initiate the grab animation, immediately cancel it with a backward dash, and watch defenders waste their stun abilities or bear traps. Once their cooldowns are triggered, you have a clear 5-to-8 second window to steal the egg uninterrupted.

## Troubleshooting Common Gameplay Issues

*   **Egg Dropping Prematurely:** Check if you are accidentally double-pressing your sprint or interact key. If your stamina runs completely out, heavy eggs will automatically drop to the floor.
*   **Stuck in Animation:** If your character freezes during a steal, press your jump button twice or tap your inventory toggle to reset character physics without disconnecting.
*   **Incubation Pausing:** Ensure your base power generator is fueled with coins. If power reaches zero, all egg timers pause until the generator is refilled.
