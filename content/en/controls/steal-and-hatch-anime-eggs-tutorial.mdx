## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most important control to master in this Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs tutorial?
The most critical mechanic is mastering sprint toggling and jump timing (`Left Shift` and `Spacebar` on PC). Maintaining stamina while navigating tight corners around sleeping bosses prevents alarm triggers and guarantees safe extractions.

### Why is my character moving slowly while carrying an egg?
Eggs possess weight modifiers based on their rarity tier. Heavy Mythic and Boss eggs apply an automatic movement penalty to your base WalkSpeed. You can counteract this by upgrading your treadmill stats and equipping high-tier agility trails.

### Should I hatch duplicate pets or sell them immediately?
If you have unlocked the fusion station, hold onto duplicate rare and epic pets to craft higher-tier golden variants. If you are struggling with pen storage space, sell duplicates below rare tier to fund immediate pen upgrades.

### Can other players steal eggs from my personal plot pen?
Once an egg is safely deposited inside your plot pen, rival players cannot steal it unless your plot security locks are disabled. Always upgrade your plot barriers early to prevent base intrusions.
