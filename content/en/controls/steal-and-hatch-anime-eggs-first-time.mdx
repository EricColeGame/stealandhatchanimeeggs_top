## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs first time without getting eliminated?
To **Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs first time** without dying, scout your extraction route before picking up the egg. Use your sprint key (**Shift** on PC) only when clearing open fields, and weave around map obstacles to break the line of sight of aggressive opponents.

### How often do eggs respawn in biomes?
Eggs refresh every in-game night cycle, which occurs exactly every 4.5 minutes. When the cycle resets, new egg sizes and rarity tiers appear in nests across all unlocked regions.

### Can all special eggs be earned through normal gameplay?
Almost all eggs, including Rift, Cherry Blossom, and Monster Eggs, can be stolen and hatched in-game. However, select novelty eggs, such as Brainrot Eggs, are store exclusives that require Robux.

### What should I do if another player is guarding a nest?
If a nest is guarded, do not contest it directly without high-speed pets. Rotate to an adjacent biome, grab a lower-tier egg to build your stats, or wait for the 4.5-minute reset to scatter competitors across different zones.
