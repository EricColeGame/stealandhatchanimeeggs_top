In the current game loop, characters function primarily as passive income generators for your tycoon base. Rather than battling other players directly, their main purpose is generating cash per second, filling your collection index, and financing character upgrades and Speed training.

### Do size multipliers and mutation buffs stack on the same character?
Yes. Size rolls and mutation modifiers roll separately upon hatching and stack multiplicatively. For example, a unit with a Large size bonus and a Spirit Bloom mutation will apply both increases to its base rarity payout.

### How do I identify a character if the name doesn't match official anime titles?
Most units use original styling, unique tags, and silhouette designs inspired by popular anime tropes rather than direct licensed names. Identify your units by reading their rarity color, active stat tags, and mutation labels directly above their heads.

### Should I keep every character I hatch?
You should hatch and record every unique unit to fill out your collection index for permanent account perks. However, you should only park high-earning units on your active plot slots, keeping lower-tier duplicates stored in your inventory.
