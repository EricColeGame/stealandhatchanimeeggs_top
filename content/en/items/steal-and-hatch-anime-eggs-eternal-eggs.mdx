## How to Outrun Guardians and Steal Eternal Eggs Safely

Egg guardians sleep directly beside their nests until disturbed. Snatching high-tier loot instantly draws aggro. If a guardian strikes you before you reach base, you drop the egg and lose your haul.

### Essential Survival Strategies

* **Upgrade on Treadmills Continuously:** Never head into advanced biomes like Cosmic, Titan Temple, or Rift without investing your coins back into treadmill training and equipped trails.
* **Master the 5-Minute Reset Cycle:** Nests reset every 5 minutes. Position yourself at high-value spawns (such as Volcano or Prehistoric) right before the reset to grab **Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs eternal eggs** the second they materialize.
* **Bait Guardian Pathing:** Circle the nest slightly before grabbing the egg. Lure the guardian's sleeping posture away from your primary exit vector to buy precious lead time.
* **Coordinate Distractions:** In multiplayer lobbies, player experience suggests having a teammate provoke the guardian first, creating a safe window to grab the heavier egg unhindered.
