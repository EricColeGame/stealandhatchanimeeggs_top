## Core Mechanics: How Egg Extraction and Hatching Works

The foundational loop of the game revolves around venturing into hostile territory, grabbing an egg right under a boss's nose, and booking it back to your safe zone. Each biome features a dedicated Guardian tasked with defending its nests.

```
[Enter Biome] ➜ [Locate Egg Spawn] ➜ [Outrun Guardian] ➜ [Deposit in Pen] ➜ [Hatch Pet]
```

Every egg displays distinct visual patterns and color palettes. As you log more hours, you will quickly identify what creature rests inside long before finishing the incubation cycle. Once you bring your prize past the safe boundary line and place it in your pen, the hatching countdown begins.

### Key Rules for Safe Extraction
* **Watch Aggro Triggers:** Grabbing an egg instantly draws the full attention of the local Guardian.
* **Sprint Momentum:** Never grab an egg while standing still; build momentum before initiating the pickup interact prompt.
* **Pen Capacity Management:** Clear out low-tier common duplicates frequently so your base pen always has room for high-value drops.
