## How to Defeat the Abyss Overlord Boss

The Abyss Overlord serves as the centerpiece raid of the Rift update. Every 30 minutes, a global alert flashes across the screen 30 seconds prior to the boss portal opening. The purple rift portal can materialize across various biomes, including late-game zones such as the Titan Temple and Cherry Blossom areas.

```
[30s Warning Alert] ➔ [Find Purple Rift Portal] ➔ [Phase 1: Destroy Crystals] ➔ [Phase 2: Attack Exposed Arms] ➔ [Collect Boss Tokens]
```

### Combat Phases and Tactics

1. **Phase 1: Power Crystal Destruction**
   * The Overlord is completely immune to direct damage while its shielding crystals are active.
   * Spread out across the arena to dodge incoming area-of-effect (AoE) attacks.
   * Focus fire on each crystal until all defensive nodes are shattered.

2. **Phase 2: Slam Window & Arm Vulnerability**
   * After the crystals fall, the Overlord initiates massive ground-slam attacks.
   * Evade the initial impact zone, then immediately target the exposed arms while they remain stationary on the arena floor.
   * Repeat this cycle until the boss's entire health bar is depleted.

According to community reports, fighting in well-populated public servers drastically cuts down clear times, allowing players to secure their rewards with minimal risk.

### Abyss Overlord Rewards Breakdown

Each victory over the Abyss Overlord grants a substantial payout:

* **Boss Tokens:** 1,200 to 1,500 base tokens per victory.
* **Mastery Progress:** Direct credit toward your account-wide Boss Mastery tier.

## Rift Machine Sacrifices: How to Get Rift Eggs

Located in the main lobby, the Rift Machine allows players to trade existing inventory pets for exclusive event eggs. To initiate a sacrifice, you must provide the three specific pets requested on the machine's display board.

```
[Inspect Lobby Machine] ➔ [Submit 3 Requested Pets] ➔ [Pets Consumed] ➔ [Receive Random Rift Egg]
```

### Sacrifice Drop Rates

When you complete a sacrifice ritual, the machine randomly yields one of three special eggs:

| Reward Egg | Drop Probability | Primary Hatch Pool Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Riftborn Egg** | 45% | Common to Rare event fighters |
| **Riftbeasts Egg** | 35% | Epic to Legendary creatures |
| **Shattered Rift Egg** | 20% | Mythic & Divine units (e.g., Shattered Colossus) |

### Key Sacrifice Rules

* **Permanent Loss:** Sacrificed pets are permanently deleted from your inventory. Never sacrifice your primary income-earning companions.
* **3-Hour Rotation:** The requested pet recipe updates every 3 hours alongside global banner rotations.
* **Recipe Rerolls:** If the machine requests rare or difficult-to-obtain units, you can spend a reroll token to generate a new pet requirement list.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest way to get tokens in the Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs rift update?
The fastest method is to defeat the Abyss Overlord raid boss every 30 minutes and push your Boss Mastery to Level 5. Level 5 provides a permanent +50% Token boost, increasing your payout from 1,200–1,500 tokens to upwards of 2,250 tokens per clear.

### Can I get my pets back after using the Rift Machine?
No. All pet sacrifices submitted to the Rift Machine are permanently consumed. Make sure you only submit duplicate or spare units that are not part of your active income team.

### How often do the Rift Banners rotate?
Rift Banners rotate globally across all game servers every 3 hours. When the timer expires, the active egg pool and the Rift Machine's sacrifice recipe change simultaneously for all players.

### What does the Fractured mutation do?
The Fractured mutation is a special consumable bought from the Boss Shop for 400 Boss Tokens. Applying it to an active egg permanently increases the income multiplier of the pet that hatches from it.
