## Advanced Escape Tactics & Pro Tips

Mastering the mechanics of **Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs eggs** requires smart pathfinding and sharp reflexes during chases.

### 1. Bait the Boss's Heavy Attack
Most bosses perform a wind-up attack when you first enter their aggro circle. Bait this animation before grabbing the egg, giving you a 2–3 second window where the boss is immobilized.

### 2. Cut Tight Corners
Bosses have wide turning radii. Running in sharp zig-zags around map geometry causes the boss to lose pathfinding momentum, allowing you to slip away even with lower speed stats.

### 3. Maintain Speed Reserves
Avoid stamina exhaustion by pacing your sprint bursts if you are navigating maps with terrain hazards or elevated jumps.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What happens if a boss catches me while carrying an egg?
If a boss catches you during an extraction run, you will faint and respawn at your base plot. You will lose the stolen egg, but your character stats, speed progress, and stored cash remain safe.

### How do I hatch Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs eggs faster?
You can reduce hatch times by upgrading your base plot level, unlocking specific plot perks, or using temporary speed-boost consumables available in the in-game shop.

### Can other players steal my egg while I am running?
Based on current game mechanics, other players cannot steal an egg directly from your hands while you are returning to your plot. However, they can lure bosses toward your path, so always stay alert during high-traffic runs.

### What is the fastest way to earn cash early in the game?
The best early-game strategy is to run continuous sprints in World 1 until you have filled all starting plot slots with Rare fighters. Use those earnings exclusively to upgrade your treadmill to Level 2 before attempting World 2 runs.
