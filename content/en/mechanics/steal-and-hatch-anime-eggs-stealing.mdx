## Core Mechanics of Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs Stealing

At its heart, the game turns standard pet collection into an adrenaline-fueled stealth heist. Instead of simply buying eggs from a shop dispenser, you must venture out of your safe plot, trespass into hostile enemy territories, grab an egg, and haul it back home.


### The Heist Loop Explained

1. **Infiltration**: You enter a designated anime-themed zone where powerful bosses rest or patrol.
2. **Egg Selection**: You locate high-value eggs scattered around the sleeping or roaming boss.
3. **The Grab**: Interacting with an egg attaches it to your character, significantly slowing you down or restricting mobility unless you have proper speed boosts.
4. **The Alarm & Chase**: Snatching an egg immediately sounds an alarm, waking or alerting the zone boss.
5. **Plot Placement**: You must navigate terrain hazards and avoid boss attacks to place the stolen item safely into your pen.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most efficient way to practice Steal and Hatch Anime Eggs stealing?
The best approach is to focus on Zone 1 and Zone 2 until your treadmill training allows you to outpace starter bosses. Focus on nearest-egg extraction to build up gold for your first pen upgrades before attempting deep runs for rare eggs.

### How do I stop bosses from catching me when carrying heavy eggs?
Egg weight applies a movement speed debuff. You can counteract this by purchasing higher-tier speed trails, leveling your character's agility on treadmills, or unlocking Rebirth perks that reduce carrying encumbrance.

### What should I do if my pen is full of eggs?
Upgrade your pen level immediately to unlock additional slots and speed up incubation timers. If you lack the coins for an upgrade, sell unwanted common eggs or lower-tier hatched pets to clear essential space.

### Do index rewards carry over after rebirthing?
Yes. Based on player experience and general progression mechanics, all index completions and permanent stat milestones remain unlocked after you perform a rebirth.
