### Step-by-Step Setup Process

Follow these steps to establish your own VIP realm:

1. **Navigate to the Game Page**: Search for the official experience in your browser or desktop app.
2. **Open the Servers Section**: Scroll down below the play button and description to find the **Servers** tab.

4. **Configure Settings**: Click the three dots next to your newly created server, select **Configure**, and choose whether friends can join automatically.
