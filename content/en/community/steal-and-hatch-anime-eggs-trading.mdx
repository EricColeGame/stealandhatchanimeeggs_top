## Step-by-Step Guide to Safe and Profitable Trading

Trading in fast-paced multiplayer hubs can be hectic. If you jump into trades blindly, you risk accepting deceptive offers. Following a structured exchange routine keeps your inventory safe while optimizing profit margins.

```
Identify Target Pet ➔ Check Recent Market Values ➔ Enter Trade Hub ➔ Verify Item Details ➔ Confirm Both Trade Windows
```

### Essential Steps for Every Trade Session

1. **Audit Your Inventory**: Group your pets into "Keepers," "High-Value Trade Bait," and "Bulk Add-ons."
2. **Scout Server Values**: Spend 5 to 10 minutes observing chat offers to gauge current buying and selling pressures.
3. **Initiate Direct Requests**: Approach players offering the exact units you need rather than spamming generic trade requests.
4. **Double-Check Stats**: Hover over the offered pet in the trade menu to verify it is the exact variant (e.g., Shiny vs. Normal) agreed upon.
5. **Execute the Trade**: Allow the countdown timer to finish without rushing to ensure the other party hasn't swapped items at the last second.

| Phase | Recommended Action | Common Mistake to Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Preparation** | Lock your valuable equipped pets | Leaving mythics unlocked in bag |

## Common Trading Pitfalls and How to Avoid Scams

Because the trading scene moves quickly, bad actors often attempt trick trades. Staying vigilant protects your hard-earned progress.

* **The Quick-Switch Scam**: A trader places a high-tier Shiny Mythic in the trade window, chatters to distract you, cancels, reopens, and swaps it for a standard version. Always inspect the item card before clicking final confirm.
* **Trust Trades and Multiplying Glitches**: Never give pets to another player under the promise that they will "dupe" them or return them with bonus stats. There are no legitimate pet duplication mechanics.
* **Third-Party Script & External Link Schemes**: Community reports frequently highlight shady links promising free pet scripts or auto-traders. Third-party automation tools can compromise your account security and lead to permanent bans.

| Scam Type | Red Flag Indicator | Prevention Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fast Swap** | Rapidly canceling and reopening trade | Re-verify item card every time |
| **Duplication Claim** | "Give me your pet to copy it" | Never trade without receiving equal value |
| **External Links** | Promising script downloads or keys | Keep all interactions inside official game hubs |
| **Overpay Promises** | "Accept this junk, I'll pay on alt" | Reject multi-trade promises |
